Delete your LogM8 account or data

LogM8 - ATO Logbook (app by LogM8, Adelaide, Australia)

Option 1 - Delete your account in the app

  1. Open LogM8 (Android app or logm8.com.au/app.html) and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and all logbook data are deleted immediately from our database.

Option 2 - Email us

Send an email from the address you signed up with to viny@logm8.com.au with the subject "Delete my LogM8 account". We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

If you only want some of your data removed (for example one vehicle's logbook or your receipt images) but want to keep your account, say so in the email and we will delete just that data.

What is deleted

  • Your login (email / Google sign-in link) and profile
  • All trips, odometer readings, vehicles and logbook settings
  • All expenses and receipt images
  • Your subscription record in our database (your Google Play or Stripe subscription itself is cancelled by you in Google Play or through the app's billing portal)

What is kept

Minimal billing records (invoice number, amount, date) may be kept for up to 5 years where Australian tax law requires it. They are not linked to your logbook data. Nothing else is retained.

See also our Privacy Policy.
