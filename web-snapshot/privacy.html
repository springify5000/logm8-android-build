Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

LogM8 ("we", "us") is an Australian vehicle logbook app available at logm8.com.au and on Google Play. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have. We handle personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address and name when you sign up with email or Google Sign-In.
  • Logbook data you enter: trips (start and end times, distances, odometer readings, trip purpose and description), vehicle details (make, model, registration), expenses and receipt images you choose to upload, and your logbook settings.
  • Location data: when you start a trip with GPS tracking, the app collects your device location while the trip is running, to calculate distance and record the route. Location is not collected in the background when no trip is active. You can log trips manually without enabling location.
  • Payment information: subscriptions on the web are processed by Stripe and on Android by Google Play. We never see or store your full card number. We store your subscription status and plan.
  • Technical data: basic device and usage information (app version, device type, error logs) needed to run and improve the service.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the logbook service: record trips, calculate distances and business-use percentages, and generate reports and exports (CSV, PDF, ATO-style logbook templates).
  • To manage your account, free trial and subscription, and to send service emails (for example, payment failures or important changes).
  • To fix bugs, prevent abuse and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use your logbook data for advertising.

3. Where your data is stored

Your account and logbook data are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Authentication), operated by Google LLC, with data hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure that may be located outside Australia. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is restricted to your own account.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase - authentication, database and hosting of your data.
  • Stripe - web payments and subscription billing.
  • Google Play Billing and RevenueCat - Android subscriptions.
  • Google Sign-In - optional login method.

Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy and only to provide its service to us.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can export your data at any time from the app. You can delete your account and all associated logbook data from the Profile screen in the app, or by emailing us at the address below; deletion is completed within 30 days. We may keep minimal billing records where required by Australian tax law.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your personal information at any time through the app or by contacting us. If you have a complaint about how we handle your information, contact us first; if you are not satisfied you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

7. Children

LogM8 is intended for drivers and business users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at logm8.com.au/privacy.html.
